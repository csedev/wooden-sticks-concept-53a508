Design system

Fairway editorial

A quiet, magazine-like resort site where the club’s own drone photography fills the page and EB Garamond headings sit in generous warm-white space, like a well-set yardage book. Deep clubhouse green carries a few emphasis bands, bronze is kept only for actions, and every section holds one idea. The famous holes and the all-inclusive Player’s Badge are stated plainly, in concrete hole numbers and tee names, not in decoration.

04

Course hero

Home and the main landing pages (Golf, The Course, Accommodations). A full-bleed aerial marked data-hero, 88svh on desktop (min 600px, 72svh on phones), using only wide photos that fit a hero (M5, M11, M12, M14, M24, M26, M30). The h1 in EB Garamond 500, slightly tightened, sits lower left within the 12-column grid on a soft bottom-left gradient shade strong enough for 4.5:1 white text, with one line of intro, a bronze Book a Tee Time button and a ghost secondary button (Plan a Stay & Play Trip). In place of a scroll cue, a small tracked text link anchors to the next section (for example, See the inspired holes); no arrow and no animation. Shown at the top of this page, as it sits under the header.

The course

A Ron Garl design, played from six tee sets

Before you book

Questions about your round

Do I need to be a member to play?

No. Wooden Sticks is open to the public. Anyone can book a tee time online or by phone, and no membership is needed.

What does the green fee include?

Every green fee is an all-inclusive Player’s Badge, which covers:

  • 18 holes on the course
  • A shared power cart
  • Use of the practice facilities
  • A post-round meal at Fairways Dining
Is it cheaper to book online?

Yes. Tee times booked online come with a lower rate than those booked by phone. You can see every rate on the green fee rates page.

Can I stay overnight at the course?

Yes. There are six two-bedroom cabins and eight one-bedroom suites on site. Book them with a 36-hole Stay & Play package or as accommodation only.

What happens if it rains during my round?

If play is stopped because of weather, the golf shop will issue a rain check for the holes you could not complete.

Confirm the rain check rules (holes played, how credit is calculated and how long it is valid).
