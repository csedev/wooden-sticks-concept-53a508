The Player’s Badge

What your Player’s Badge includes

  • 01

    18 holes of golf

    A full round on a Ron Garl design, including holes inspired by #12 and #13 at Augusta, the Postage Stamp at Troon and #17 at Sawgrass.

    See the hole-by-hole tour
  • 02

    Shared power cart

    A cart for every two players. The course covers more than 330 acres, so the drive between holes is part of the round.

  • 03

    Practice facilities

    Warm up before you play on the driving range and the putting and chipping greens.

  • 04

    Post-round meal

    Sit down in the dining room or on the patio after your round. We make the reservation for you.

Want to eat before your round?

A meal before play is at your own expense, so reserve a table at Fairways Dining to make sure you tee off on time.

Appetizers, desserts, alcohol, on-course food and drink, and pro shop purchases are extra.

Supply a brighter photo of the Fairways Dining room or patio; the current shot is underexposed with the window light blown out.

Booking terms

Before you book

Payment is taken in full when you book your tee time.

Changes are credited toward another round, not refunded. Rain checks follow our rain check policy.

Single players cannot book. Twosomes may be paired with another group, and public tee times run until 3:00 pm.
