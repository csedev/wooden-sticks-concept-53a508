Hole-by-hole course tour

Play golf’s most famous holes in one round

Amen Corner at Augusta. The Road Hole at St. Andrews. The island green at Sawgrass. At Wooden Sticks you can play holes inspired by all three, plus Troon, Carnoustie, Oakmont and Pine Valley, in a single round in Uxbridge, Ontario.

Inspired by the greats

A tribute to golf’s history and traditions

Architect Ron Garl designed Wooden Sticks as a tribute to the history and traditions of the game. His layout mixes original holes with holes inspired by famous originals at Oakmont, Augusta National, Pine Valley, St. Andrews, Troon, Carnoustie and TPC Sawgrass.

During your round you will play off a road in Scotland, across a famous creek in Georgia, onto an island in Florida and through pine forests in New Jersey. The original holes hold their own: many visitors tell us they like them just as much as the inspired ones.

The 11th hole has two options, 11A and 11B, each inspired by a different par 3 at Troon. Six tee sets, from Oak at the back to Spruce at the front, let you choose the length that suits your game. Course ratings and slopes for every tee are on the scorecard.

Your round

Play the tour yourself

Amen Corner, the Postage Stamp, the Road Hole and the island green, all in one afternoon, about an hour from Toronto. Wooden Sticks is open to the public, and your Player’s Badge is all-inclusive: 18 holes, a shared power cart, use of the practice facilities and a meal after your round.
